# Local SEO Strategy for Kalol & Gandhinagar

## Executive Summary
SK Air Condition targets two high-potential markets in Gujarat with minimal competition. This strategy focuses on Google My Business optimization, local content, and location-specific landing pages.

---

## Part 1: Market Analysis

### Kalol Opportunity
- **Market Size**: ~50,000 residents
- **Competition**: LOW (very few professional AC repair services)
- **Keyword Difficulty**: LOW
- **Business Potential**: HIGH

### Gandhinagar Opportunity
- **Market Size**: ~200,000 residents
- **Competition**: MEDIUM (growing market)
- **Keyword Difficulty**: LOW-MEDIUM
- **Business Potential**: VERY HIGH (capital city)

## Part 4: Citation Building (Local Authority)

### High-Priority Citations (Do First)
1. **Google My Business** - Critical for local search
   - Create separate GMB for each location if possible
   - Or use one GMB with multiple service areas

2. **JustDial** (Most Important in India)
   - Register with "AC Repair" category
   - Add both Kalol and Gandhinagar locations
   - Include phone, address, hours, services

3. **India Yellow Pages** (justdial.com alternative)
   - Add comprehensive business listing
   - Include service area coverage

4. **99acres.com** (Property sites - relevant for B2B referrals)
   - List under AC repair/maintenance services

5. **IndiaMART** - Local business directory
   - Helps with local authority building

### Medium-Priority (Do Second Wave)
- Local Gandhinagar business directories
- Kalol municipality/chamber of commerce websites
- Regional Gujarat business listings
- Industry-specific HVAC directories
