## 🔴 PRIORITY 1: Search Console Setup (DO TODAY - 15 minutes)

### Step 1: Add Property to Google Search Console
**Time: 3 minutes**

```
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Start now" or "+ Add property"
3. Select "URL prefix" option
4. Enter: https://sk-air-condition.vercel.app/
5. Click "Continue"
```

### Step 2: Verify Ownership (Method: HTML Tag - Auto-detect)
**Time: 2 minutes**

```
Your verification meta tag is ALREADY in index.html:
<meta name="google-site-verification" 
      content="EAD2KbK1vDZeOt-6ntjnntK7SoUIfTyeodkWawF2Xtk" />

✓ Google will auto-detect this
✓ Click "Verify" in Search Console
✓ Wait for confirmation (usually instant)
```

### Step 3: Submit Sitemap
**Time: 2 minutes**

```
1. After verification, go to "Sitemaps" (left sidebar)
2. Under "Add a new sitemap"
3. Enter: sitemap.xml
4. Click "Submit"
5. Status shows: "Sitemaps submitted"
```

**Time: 3 minutes**

```

3. Click "Request indexing"

5. Google crawls within 24 hours

Repeat for:
- /services

- /about

## 🟢 PRIORITY 3: Build Quality Backlinks (Slow & Steady)

### What are Backlinks?
"Other websites linking to your site = Votes of confidence to Google"

### How Google Views Backlinks:
```
1 link from high-authority site = Better than 100 from low sites
Quality > Quantity
Natural links > Manufactured links
```

### Step 1: DIY Backlink Strategy (No cost, takes time = BEST)

#### 1a: Directory Backlinks (Easy - Week 1)
```
Submit to these free business directories:
(Each submission = 1 backlink!)

✓ Local Ahmedabad directories
✓ Indian business directories  
✓ Industry-specific directories
✓ Google My Business (already doing)

Time: 2-3 hours
Backlinks gained: 5-10
Quality: Medium

Directories to submit to:
- Business.com
- Yelp.com (international, but good)
- Local Ahmedabad chamber of commerce
- Gujarat business listings
```

#### 1b: Resource Page Backlinks (Medium - Week 2-3)
```
Find local "Best AC Repair Companies" pages and ask to be listed:

Outreach template:
"Hi [Website Owner],

I found your article: '[Article Title]'
It's a great resource for Ahmedabad residents!

We think SK Air Condition would be a great addition 
since we offer professional AC repair.

Would you consider adding us to your list?
[Your website URL]

Contact: +91 7802 984 111

Thank you!"

Expected response rate: 10-20%
Each positive response = 1 quality backlink
```

#### 1c: Local Business Network (Best Quality - Ongoing)
```
Contact local businesses you work with:

"Hi [Business Owner],

Example link text:
"[Business Name] recommends SK Air Condition for AC repair"

This helps your customers know your partners!"

Natural, high-quality backlinks!
```

#### 1d: Guest Post Backlinks (High Quality but Hard - Month 2+)

```
